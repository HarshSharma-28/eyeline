Eyeline for Mac
===============

Which download?  Apple menu > About This Mac.
  "Chip: Apple M1/M2/M3/M4..."  -> Apple Silicon version
  "Processor: Intel..."          -> Intel version

Install (about 2 minutes, only once)
1. Unzip, then drag Eyeline.app into your Applications folder.
2. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line and press Return:

   xattr -cr /Applications/Eyeline.app && codesign --force --deep --sign - /Applications/Eyeline.app

   Why: Eyeline isn't from the App Store, so macOS blocks it until it is unblocked
   and signed on your own Mac. This line does both, using Apple's own built-in tools.
3. Open Eyeline from Applications. When macOS asks for microphone access, click Allow.

Using it
  The overlay floats at the top of your screen, just under the camera, and stays
  above full-screen apps too. Drag it by the move button or the empty parts of its bars.

Shortcuts (work in any app)
  Cmd+Shift+Space        start / pause
  Cmd+Shift+Up/Down      back / forward a line
  Cmd+Shift+Left/Right   scroll speed (Auto mode)
  Cmd+Shift+R            restart
  Cmd+Shift+E            open the Eyeline window
  Cmd+Shift+H            show / hide the overlay
  Cmd+Shift+L            turn click-through on / off

Screen recording and screen sharing on a Mac
  "Hide from screen recording & screen sharing" hides Eyeline from Google Meet in Chrome.
  On macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later, Apple no longer lets apps hide from Zoom, Teams,
  QuickTime, screenshots or recent OBS. There is no setting that changes this.
  To keep Eyeline out of a recording or a call, share or record a single window
  (for example just your browser or slides) instead of the whole screen.

Voice follow runs offline on your Mac. English (US accent) is built in.
English (Indian accent) and Hindi download once (about 35-45 MB) the first time you pick them.
