Eyeline for Windows (64-bit)
============================

1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere permanent (for example Documents\Eyeline).
2. Double-click Eyeline.exe.
   Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't code-signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Paste your script in the Eyeline window and press Start.
   If Windows asks for microphone access, allow it.

The overlay floats at the top of your main screen, right under the webcam:
  - Control bar: Start/Pause, Restart, Script, move, compact view, settings, End
  - Transcript bar: the words Eyeline hears, plus Voice / Auto
  - Script panel: your words, moving up as you speak
Drag it anywhere with the move button or the empty parts of its bars. Its position is remembered.
Clicks on the script text pass through to the app underneath.

Shortcuts (work in any app):
  Ctrl+Shift+Space       start / pause
  Ctrl+Shift+Up/Down     back / forward a line
  Ctrl+Shift+Left/Right  scroll speed (Auto mode)
  Ctrl+Shift+R           restart
  Ctrl+Shift+E           open the Eyeline window
  Ctrl+Shift+H           show / hide the overlay
  Ctrl+Shift+L           turn click-through on / off

Screen recording and screen sharing
  With "Hide from screen recordings" on (the default), both Eyeline windows are
  invisible to screen recordings, screenshots and screen sharing (OBS, Zoom, Teams,
  Google Meet, Snipping Tool, Xbox Game Bar). You still see them normally.
  The taskbar icon still shows in a whole-screen share.

Voice follow runs offline on your PC. English (US accent) is built in.
English (Indian accent) and Hindi download once (about 35-45 MB) the first time you pick them.
